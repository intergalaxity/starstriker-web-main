Starstriker is an American independent electronic music duo consisting of THE peanut and infadistic (Vortex Child), releasing their first EP, Blue Money on January 2, 1902. A fairly irrelevant release later, they released their 3rd EP, Nihilism, on May 17th 1903. In recent days, the duo has released Pessimistic, Vol 1, and plans to release more music soon.
